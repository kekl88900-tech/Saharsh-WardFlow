Security & safety

  • Clinical staff determine patient severity.
  • Resource matching is a coordination aid.
  • Critical pre-emption requires an authorised demo role and explicit confirmation.
  • Active resource conflicts are blocked by application logic.
  • Role selector is not real authentication.
  • No backend, central database or multi-user synchronization is configured.
  • Not approved for real patient care; do not enter real patient information.
